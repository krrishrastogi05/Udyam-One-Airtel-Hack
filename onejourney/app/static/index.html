Business identity verificationWhat happens to your document

We process the uploaded synthetic business document to extract relevant fields and compare them with the supplied mock registries. Documents are handled locally in this demonstration, not transmitted to a real government service. Data is not used to train a model. OCR previews mask registration IDs. Journey records and extracted OCR results expire after 7 days; use “Delete my data” to erase them sooner. Images are processed in memory and are not stored.
